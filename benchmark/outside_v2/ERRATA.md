## Post-freeze: standing-condition rule (2026-11-28)

Rule fixed by the team before any reading of v2: `expected_alone` may assume a condition the sentence does not state
only if the source prints it at the start of the paper (abstract, introduction, or the opening of the first
technical section before its first subsection), at the start of the section the item is in, or in the theorem
block (its statement and the sentence introducing it). The audit (`STANDING.md`, `standing_conditions.jsonl`)
found 20 items whose frozen `expected_alone` leaned on a condition with no such location. Each is re-derived below
without that condition. `items.jsonl` is unchanged; these values replace the frozen `expected_alone` for these
items. `expected_with_hypotheses` is not affected (it already adds every hypothesis the source states anywhere).

Counterexamples marked "ours" were worked out during this audit; the numerical ones were run with a short NumPy
script (not kept in the repo), and the numbers are quoted.
